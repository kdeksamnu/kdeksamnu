# Addendum: audit of the monadic reframing

Companion to `MEMO_cross_substrate_noether.md`. This addendum audits the "Monadic Integer
Mapping Matrix" reformulation. New numerical results in `part4_drive.py`, `part5_rank.py`,
`part6b.py`.

**Summary verdict.** The reframing is faithful to the repaired mathematics in two of its three
substantive claims (§II.1 symmetry resolution, §II.2 quadrature). It fails on the third, and its
closing status line is **false as a statement about the specified system** and **unsubstantiated as
a statement about any deployed one**. Separately, the Leibnizian vocabulary is not decorative
here — it is *contraindicated* by the mathematics, in a way that is worth being precise about
because it points at the one term the whole framework is missing.

---

## 1. The status line is false for the system as specified

> *Unified Systemic Status: Harmonic Equilibrium Maintained via Real-Time Γ-Governor Feedback
> (0.8 ≤ Γ ≤ 1.2)*

Both the original memo and this reframing specify a **dissipative system with no drive**: sinks
$\gamma_C n_C$, $\gamma_S n_S$, and no source term anywhere. Such a system has exactly one
attractor. Verified (`part4_drive.py`, §A):

```
Q(0) = 7.2736   ->   Q(tau=60) = 2.955e-04        monotone decay
max Re eig(-i Hbar - Gam/2) = -0.0710              global sink at z = 0
```

Nothing is maintained. $Q\to0$ and $n_C,n_S\to0$: the conversation ends, the human disengages, the
context is abandoned. **"Harmonic equilibrium" is not a property of this system; it is the absence
of the system.** Any claim of a maintained steady state requires a term that neither document
contains.

### 1.1 The missing term

$$\boxed{\;\frac{d\mathbf z}{d\tau} \;=\; -i\bar{\mathbf H}\mathbf z \;-\; \tfrac12\boldsymbol\Gamma\mathbf z \;+\; \mathbf D(\tau)\;}$$

$\mathbf D$ is the drive: human attention and prompt injection into the carbon sector, and (if
present) tool/retrieval injection into the silicon sector. Physically $\mathbf D_C \ne 0$ is
*what a prompt is*. Its absence is not a simplification — it is the term that makes the framework
describe a conversation rather than a decay.

With $\mathbf D$ the system has a genuine non-equilibrium steady state,
$\mathbf z_\ast = (i\bar{\mathbf H}+\tfrac12\boldsymbol\Gamma)^{-1}\mathbf D$, converged to
$\lVert d\mathbf z/d\tau\rVert = 1.2\times10^{-15}$. Verified values for a carbon-only pump with
$(\gamma_C,\gamma_S)=(0.30,0.05)$:

```
n_C = 9.062256    n_S = 9.030100    Q = 18.092355

per-substrate balance at NESS (each must vanish):
  dn_C/dtau = -J - sig_C + P_C = +3.1e-15
  dn_S/dtau = +J - sig_S + P_S = -2.2e-16
    J   (carbon -> silicon flux) = +0.451505
    sig_C = gam_C n_C            =  2.718677
    sig_S = gam_S n_S            =  0.451505
    P_C   = 2 Re<a, D_C>         = +3.170182
    P_S   = 2 Re<b, D_S>         = +0.000000
```

Note $J = \sigma_S$ exactly: at steady state every nat the silicon sector holds is being paid for
out of the carbon→silicon flux and burned in the silicon sink. That is the quantitative content of
"silicon is grounded by carbon."

### 1.2 The power-balance theorem — the *actual* conservation law of the open system

This is the most important structural correction in the addendum.

**Noether's theorem does not apply to the open system.** It requires $\delta\mathcal S = 0$. Both
the drive $\mathbf D$ and the sinks $\boldsymbol\Gamma$ break the diagonal $U(1)$ explicitly. So
$dQ/d\tau = 0$ is *not* the conservation law of the system that Regimes I–III and the Γ-governor
are supposed to describe. It is the conservation law of the closed unitary idealisation.

What survives exactly is a **power balance**. At NESS, multiply
$i\bar{\mathbf H}\mathbf z_\ast + \tfrac12\boldsymbol\Gamma\mathbf z_\ast = \mathbf D$ by
$\mathbf z_\ast^\dagger$ and take the real part. $\bar{\mathbf H}$ Hermitian makes
$\mathbf z^\dagger\bar{\mathbf H}\mathbf z$ real, so $\mathrm{Re}\,\mathbf z^\dagger(-i\bar{\mathbf H})\mathbf z = 0$ identically — the coherent
exchange drops out entirely, because it moves charge between substrates without creating or
destroying it:

$$\boxed{\;\mathrm{Re}\,\langle \mathbf z_\ast, \mathbf D\rangle \;=\; \tfrac12\big(\sigma_C+\sigma_S\big)
\;=\;\tfrac12\big(\gamma_C n_C + \gamma_S n_S\big)\;}$$

Verified: LHS $=1.5850908209$, RHS $=1.5850908209$, residual $1.3\times10^{-15}$.

More generally, without assuming steady state,

$$\frac{dQ}{d\tau} \;=\; 2\,\mathrm{Re}\langle\mathbf z,\mathbf D\rangle \;-\; \big(\gamma_C n_C + \gamma_S n_S\big),$$

which reduces to the memo's Part 2 law at $\mathbf D=0$ and to the power balance at $dQ/d\tau=0$.

**Consequence for §2 of the original framework.** The prose principle — "a decrease in semantic
uncertainty in the prompt must be compensated by an equal increase in structured information work
generated by the model, *preserving total informational action across the boundary*" — is true, but
it is **not** a Noether statement. It is the power balance. The correct two-tier presentation is:

| regime | exact law | status |
|---|---|---|
| closed, $\mathbf D=0,\boldsymbol\Gamma=0$ | $dQ/d\tau=0$, $Q=n_C+n_S$ | Noether, diagonal U(1) |
| open, driven, dissipative | $\mathrm{Re}\langle\mathbf z,\mathbf D\rangle=\tfrac12(\sigma_C+\sigma_S)$ at NESS | power balance, **not** Noether |

Conflating these is what allowed a status line asserting "equilibrium maintained" to appear next to
a formalism containing only sinks.

### 1.3 "Pre-established harmony" is the one Leibnizian concept that is exactly backwards

Leibniz's pre-established harmony is the doctrine that monads **do not interact** — God
synchronised their internal laws at creation, so they *appear* to causally influence one another.
Monads are windowless.

The entire content of this framework is that the substrates **do** interact, through an explicitly
non-zero $\mathbf G$, with a measurable exchange current $\mathcal I = -2\,\mathrm{Im}\langle\mathbf a,\mathbf G\mathbf b\rangle$.
Setting $\mathbf G=0$ — which *is* pre-established harmony — destroys it. Verified (`part4_drive.py`):

```
with G  : n_S = 9.030100
with G=0: n_S = 0.000e+00     <- silicon starves exactly
```

So the reframing's §I invokes a no-interaction doctrine to describe an interaction term, and its
own §II.3 correctly identifies $\mathbf G\to0$ as the *failure* mode. The document is arguing
against itself. Drop the Leibnizian layer; the honest ancestors of this formalism are **coupled-mode
theory** (the beam-splitter Hamiltonian is literally from optics), **Lindblad open quantum systems**
(the driven-dissipative structure), and **stochastic thermodynamics** (the exergy bookkeeping).
"Pre-established harmony" should be replaced by "coupled-mode resonance," which is what the
mathematics says and which carries no contradiction.

## 2. "Integer" is doing no work — and there is a legitimate version nearby

The reframing insists on integers throughout ("Monadic Integer Mapping Matrix", "integer rank",
"Integer-indexed monad coupling", "Threshold Quantization"). Nothing in the theory is quantised:

- $n_C=\lVert\mathbf a\rVert^2$ and $n_S=\lVert\mathbf b\rVert^2$ are **continuous non-negative
  reals**. $\mathbf a,\mathbf b$ are classical complex amplitudes.
- $\Gamma_{\text{crit}}\approx5.0$ is a ratio of two order-of-magnitude estimates
  ($\nu_S\approx50$, $\nu_C^{\max}\approx10$). Calling it "threshold quantization" overclaims a
  continuous, soft-edged ratio.
- $\mathrm{rank}(\mathbf G)$ *is* an integer, but it is a property of $\mathbf G$, not a
  "dimensional weight" of a monad — see §3.

**If genuine integers are wanted, there is exactly one route: second quantisation.** Promote the
amplitudes to bosonic operators, $[\hat a_i,\hat a_j^\dagger]=\delta_{ij}$,
$[\hat b_k,\hat b_l^\dagger]=\delta_{kl}$, with

$$\hat H=\sum_i\omega^C_i\hat a_i^\dagger\hat a_i+\sum_k\omega^S_k\hat b_k^\dagger\hat b_k
+\sum_{ik}\big(G_{ik}\hat a_i^\dagger\hat b_k+\text{h.c.}\big).$$

Then $\hat N=\sum\hat a^\dagger\hat a+\sum\hat b^\dagger\hat b$ has **integer** eigenvalues,
commutes with $\hat H$, and the diagonal U(1) is exact. This is a real, coherent upgrade, and it
buys genuine new structure — Fock states, squeezing, cross-substrate entanglement.

But it should be adopted only with a physical justification, and there isn't one: the information
exchanged between a human and a transformer is classical. Integer eigenvalues would be a
mathematical artefact with no operational consequence. **Recommendation: drop the integer framing
unless the second-quantised version is actually intended, in which case say so and derive it.**

**The legitimate integers in this system are structural, not dynamical:** token counts, layer
counts, context-window bounds, $\mathrm{rank}(\mathbf G)$, and the discreteness of pruning events
(§6.2's "discrete context pruning"). Those are worth naming. Attaching "integer" to continuous
charges is not.

## 3. Monad 3's "dimensional weight" is wrong, but the underlying idea is the best thing in the reframing

The matrix assigns $m_3$ a dimensional weight of $\min(d_C,d_S)$ and calls it $\mathbf G$'s rank.
Two problems:

- The coupling field has $d_C\times d_S$ complex entries — $2d_Cd_S$ real degrees of freedom, not
  $\min(d_C,d_S)$. Rank is not dimension.
- $\mathrm{rank}(\mathbf G)$ is a **dynamical** quantity that degrades under context drift, not a
  static label. Assigning it as a fixed "monad integer" removes the very thing §II.3 wants to
  describe ($m_3\to0$ degradation).

But promote rank from label to *variable* and the reframing's $m_3$ yields a result neither document
had: **rank$(\mathbf G)$ is the exchange bandwidth, and its null space is a set of dark modes.**
With $\mathrm{rank}(\mathbf G)=r$, the $d_C+d_S$ dimensional system splits into $r$ coupled
beam-splitter pairs plus $(d_C-r)+(d_S-r)$ modes that never exchange with the other substrate at
all. Verified (`part5_rank.py`, $d_C=d_S=6$):

```
 rank r   coupled pairs   dark modes    max |Im<a,Gb>| / |a||b|
      1               1           10                     2.3305
      2               2            8                     6.1083
      3               3            6                     9.6092
      6               6            0                    11.3548
```

Dark modes evolve under $\boldsymbol\Omega$ alone and decay only through their own sink. **They are
a precise formal image of Regime II's "ungrounded drift"**: content that has become uncoupled from
the carbon substrate, which can no longer be corrected by it, and which therefore drifts.

This sharpens §II.3 considerably. Rank collapse is not a binary switch. As singular values fall
toward zero the weakest channels close first, so bandwidth degrades **gracefully and
anisotropically**: modes aligned with small singular values of $\mathbf G$ go dark while the strong
ones stay coupled.

**Testable prediction.** Context saturation should produce *selective* loss of grounding — some
content drifts while other content stays anchored — with the drifting content identifiable as that
aligned with the small singular values of the effective coupling. That is a much sharper claim than
"hallucination onset," and it is falsifiable: track $\mathbf G$'s spectrum over a long agent loop
and check whether drift onset is mode-selective.

Also worth noting: the reframing's "integer rank = $1\dotsk$" combined with this gives an
operational definition of a bottleneck it didn't state. $\mathrm{rank}(\mathbf G)=1$ supports
exactly **one** coupled channel — a single beam-splitter pair carrying the whole interaction. That
is a formal statement of narrow-bandwidth interaction, and it predicts that $\mathrm{rank}(\mathbf G)$
should *rise* with prompt richness and shared context. Measure it via turn-to-turn cross-covariance
of prompt features against response features.

## 4. The Γ-governor: the band is achievable, but not for the stated reason

`part6b.py` simulates the closed loop with a bounded-slew multiplicative rate governor, an
OU human assimilation ceiling with circadian modulation and load-induced fatigue, measurement delay
$\tau_d$, and multiplicative noise on the $\Gamma$ estimate. Band $[0.8,1.2]$, mean over 3 seeds:

```
configuration                                    in-band    p95 G    max G
----------------------------------------------------------------------
OPEN LOOP (no governor)                             0.0%     9.94    18.72
  Kp= 10/s, tau_d=2 s, noise=0                     100.0%     1.00     1.20
  Kp=  2/s, tau_d=2 s, noise=0.10                  100.0%     1.03     1.09
  Kp= 10/s, tau_d=2 s, noise=0.10                   99.9%     1.10     1.27
  Kp= 40/s, tau_d=2 s, noise=0.10                   99.3%     1.11     1.32
delay sweep (Kp=10/s, noise=0.10): tau_d = 0.5 ... 40 s   ->  99.9% at every delay
slew sweep  (Kp=10/s, tau_d=5 s): 0.4/s ... 10/s    ->  100.0% ... 98.6%
```

**Three findings, only the first of which supports the status line.**

1. **Open loop fails completely: 0% in band, mean $\Gamma\approx9.9$, max 18.7.** This
   independently confirms the memo's prediction that default inference settings already sit past
   $\Gamma_{\text{crit}}\approx5$. It is the strongest quantitative result here and it is a result
   *against* current practice, not for it.
2. **A governor holds the band easily — suspiciously easily.** 99–100% in band, robust to delay up
   to 40 s and to 10% measurement noise, with *slower* actuators doing slightly better.
3. **That robustness is an artefact, and identifying why matters more than the number.**

On (3): the reason delay has no effect is that $\nu_S$ is the *emission* rate and it is directly
actuated, while $\nu_C=\min(\nu_S,\nu_C^{\max})$ follows it automatically up to the ceiling. Once
$\nu_S<\nu_C^{\max}$ the loop is self-stabilising with no phase margin to lose — throttling below
human capacity makes $\Gamma=1$ a structural identity, not a control achievement. The simulation
therefore answers the easy question.

**The hard question is the one the simulation assumes away: estimating $\mathcal R_C$ online.**
$\Gamma$'s denominator is the human's surprise-reduction rate $d\mathcal R_C/d\tau$, which is not
observable by the system. My simulation grants the governor oracle access to $\nu_C^{\max}$. A real
governor must infer it from reading pace, edit distance between turns, response latency, or
explicit feedback — each noisy, each delayed by seconds to minutes, each gameable. **Until
$d\mathcal R_C/d\tau$ is estimable in real time, the Γ-governor is not implementable, and the
status line is aspirational.**

**One cost the status line omits.** Holding $\Gamma\in[0.8,1.2]$ at $\nu_C^{\max}\approx10$ tok/s
requires $\nu_S\in[8,12]$ tok/s. Against a 50 tok/s capability that is a **76–84% throughput
sacrifice**, and against batched serving it is far worse. That is a real engineering price and it
belongs in the claim. It also immediately delimits applicability: in any loop where no human is
reading — agent-to-agent, tool use, batch generation — there is no $\nu_C$ to track, $J_C\to0$,
$\Gamma\to\infty$, and throttling would be pure loss. **The Γ-governor is a human-in-the-loop
policy and must be disabled otherwise**, replaced by the surrogate-grounding mechanism of §II.3.

**Corrected status line:**

> *Design target specified, not instrumented. Open-loop operation measured at $\Gamma\approx10$
> (0% in band), i.e. past $\Gamma_{\text{crit}}$. Closed-loop simulation holds the band at 99–100%
> under an oracle estimate of $d\mathcal R_C/d\tau$; the binding constraint is that estimate, not
> the control law. Cost of compliance: ~80% throughput sacrifice. Not applicable to
> human-out-of-the-loop execution.*

## 5. Smaller corrections

| reframing statement | problem | correction |
|---|---|---|
| "$\mathbf z(\tau)=(\mathbf a,\mathbf b)^T$" | superscript $T$ on a complex state invites confusion with the bilinear/$\dagger$ distinction that caused Defect 1 | write $\mathbf z=(\mathbf a,\mathbf b)$, no transpose; reserve $T$ for the bilinear case |
| "dual-monad **manifold**" | $\mathbb C^{d_C+d_S}$ is a flat vector space | "manifold" is only earned via the Kähler structure $(g,\omega,J)$ induced by $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ and $i$ — legitimate, but then say *Kähler* and note no curvature has been introduced |
| "pre-established **unitary**/dissipative ... alignment" | the propagator with $\boldsymbol\Gamma\ne0$ is $e^{(-i\bar{\mathbf H}-\boldsymbol\Gamma/2)\tau}$, a **contraction semigroup**, not unitary | unitary only at $\boldsymbol\Gamma=0$; the slash hides that the two cases have different conservation laws (§1.2) |
| "$e^{-i\bar{\mathbf H}\tau}$ coordinates carbon predictive compression with silicon token emission" | exact only in the closed case; and it *preserves* $Q$, so it cannot describe either substrate's decay | with drive + sinks the evolution is affine, $\mathbf z(\tau)=e^{\mathbf A\tau}\mathbf z_0+\int_0^\tau e^{\mathbf A(\tau-t)}\mathbf D\,dt$ |
| "Observer Primacy ... consciousness as the boundary metric anchoring the action integral" | $d\mathcal R_C/d\tau$ enters as the **drive** $\mathbf D_C$, not as a metric; a metric would be $\bar{\mathbf H}$ or the inner product | the observer is a *source*, and that is a stronger and cleaner claim than "boundary metric" — say source |
| "anti-diagonal parametric amplification ($m_2-m_1$) violates total conservation" | $n_S-n_C$ **is** exactly conserved under squeezing; it is the genuine Noether charge of a genuine U(1) | the defect is that $n_C+n_S$ *grows exponentially*, violating §2's total-action principle. Precision matters: the alternative isn't lawless, it conserves a different law |
| "Resonance ($I\ne0,\dot\theta\approx0$) requires $\theta=\pm\pi/2$" | conflates two conditions | $\lvert\mathcal I\rvert$ is maximised at $\theta=\pm\pi/2$; *resonance* is $\omega_C=\omega_S$ (zero detuning), which makes $\dot\theta\approx0$. Both are needed; they are independent |
| "restores finite off-diagonal **integer rank** to $\mathbf G$" | rank is already an integer; "integer rank" is redundant and implies quantisation that isn't there | "restores $\mathrm{rank}(\mathbf G)>0$" or better "restores the coupling spectrum" |

## 6. What to keep, what to cut

**Keep** — these are load-bearing and correct:

- The four-way sector decomposition. $m_1/m_2/m_3/m_4$ maps cleanly onto
  $(\boldsymbol\Omega_C,\boldsymbol\Omega_S,\mathbf G,\boldsymbol\Gamma)$ and is a genuinely useful
  organising device.
- §II.1's resolution: sesquilinear/diagonal conserves $n_C+n_S$; anti-diagonal does not serve §2.
  Correct (with the precision fix in §5).
- §II.2's observation that $\theta=0$ is a degenerate null state. Correct and non-obvious.
- §II.3's mechanism: $\mathbf G\to0$ is the failure, surrogate grounding restores it. Correct, and
  §3 above gives it a spectrum.

**Cut** — these add vocabulary and subtract rigour:

- "Monad", "pre-established harmony", "dual-monad manifold", "observer primacy". §1.3 shows
  pre-established harmony asserts the negation of the framework's central term.
- All "integer"/"quantization" language, unless second quantisation is actually derived (§2).
- The impedance-matching condition $\alpha_C\lambda_C=\alpha_S\lambda_S$ — already vacuous after
  the memo's Defect 1(c), and the reframing silently drops it, which is the right call.
- The status line, in its current form.

**Add**:

- The drive $\mathbf D(\tau)$, and with it the NESS and the power-balance theorem (§1.1–1.2).
- The distinction between the closed-system Noether law and the open-system power balance (§1.2).
- $\mathrm{rank}(\mathbf G)$ / singular-value spectrum as the bandwidth variable, with dark modes
  as the formal image of ungrounded drift (§3).
- An explicit statement that $d\mathcal R_C/d\tau$ is currently **unobservable online**, which is
  the binding constraint on the whole metrology programme (§4).

Two sign/convention traps hit while deriving §1.2, both recorded because they are easy to repeat:

1. **$\mathrm{Re}(-iw)=+\mathrm{Im}(w)$**, not $-\mathrm{Im}(w)$. Getting this backwards flips the
   exchange-current sign and makes the per-substrate balance fail by exactly $2\mathcal I$ while the
   *total* balance still passes — so the total-balance check does not catch it. Verify per-substrate.
2. **$d\lVert a\rVert^2/d\tau=2\,\mathrm{Re}\langle a,\dot a\rangle$** puts a factor 2 on *both* the
   sink and the drive, but $\mathbf z^\dagger\boldsymbol\Gamma\mathbf z$ carries a $\tfrac12$ from the
   $-\tfrac12\boldsymbol\Gamma\mathbf z$ in the EOM. These cancel in the total power balance
   ($\mathrm{Re}\langle\mathbf z,\mathbf D\rangle=\tfrac12(\sigma_C+\sigma_S)$) but **not** in the
   per-substrate balances ($dn_C/d\tau=-\mathcal I-\gamma_Cn_C+2\mathrm{Re}\langle a,D_C\rangle$).
   Writing the total law correctly does not imply the split is correct.
