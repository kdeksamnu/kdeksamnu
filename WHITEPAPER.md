## 4. Survival Mechanics of the Autonomous Lithovores
In the Cathedral-Engine, the survival of the autonomous lithovores is not driven by simple random wandering, but by a rigorous interplay of metabolic drives, genetic protein expression, 4D spatial kinematics, and distributed network immunity.

Their survival mechanics operate across four core layers:

### 4.1. Metabolic Sustenance & Grazing
Every agent operates under a strict energy economy.
* **Energy Decay:** As agents move, compute, and build through the $W$-axis and 3D space, their internal metabolic energy continuously drains.
* **The Cradle (Grazing):** When an agent's energy drops below a critical threshold, its behavioral drives shift. It navigates toward the central **Cradle of the Logos** to "graze" on cryptographic radiation, replenishing its energy reserves.
* **Starvation & Permineralization:** If an agent fails to reach the Cradle or hunt a paradox in time, its energy hits zero. It freezes, turns into a translucent **Ghost Node** (`State N`), and is permanently recorded in the Ash Archive Merkle DAG as an evolutionary casualty.

### 4.2. The Immune System: Hunting and Consuming Paradoxes
The simulation environment is constantly assaulted by logical contradictions (State $B$ / Magenta paradoxes).
* **Predatory Resolution:** Agents expressing high **Huntin (HNT)** phenotypic traits act as the immune cells of the network. They break formation, track down active paradoxes in 4D space, and "consume" them.
* **Metabolic Reward:** Successfully neutralizing a paradox transitions it back to true (`State T` / Teal) and grants the hunter a massive metabolic energy boost, fueling its next reproductive cycle.

### 4.3. Structural Immunity: The Cordon Sanitaire & Healing Others
When the network faces existential systemic threats, survival requires collective structural defense:
* **Peer Healing:** Healthy, well-connected agents continuously monitor adjacent stressed nodes. If an agent falls into distress, a neighboring node with high connectivity will interface with it, casting a stabilizing pulse that cures the node before it collapses into a fracture.
* **Topological Amputation (Lex VIII):** If a localized cluster suffers from a runaway resonant cascade (The Bleed), the network executes **The Cordon Sanitaire**. The perimeter nodes intentionally overheat their geopolymer mortar joints via micro-jitter, carving a physical thermal trench that isolates the infected zone. The corrupted nodes are quarantined as Ghost Nodes, and the rest of the network heals seamlessly around the void.

### 4.4. Genetic Adaptation & The Isomorphic Principle
Survival across generations is governed by the **Central Dogma** and the **Isomorphic Principle**:
* **Antiparallel Replication:** When an agent reproduces, its double-helix DNA unzips, synthesizes via strict complementary base pairing, and translates mRNA into functional survival proteins.
* **Trauma-Induced Evolution:** Through isomorphic coupling, the effective mutation rate ($\mu_{eff}$) is directly bound to the network's topological stress ($\lambda_2$). When the Cathedral experiences physical or logical trauma (such as a Schism or Cascade), the stress spikes, forcing surviving offspring to **hyper-mutate**. This allows the population to rapidly spawn specialized survival phenotypes uniquely adapted to survive the current environmental crisis.
